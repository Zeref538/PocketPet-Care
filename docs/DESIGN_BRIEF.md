# Visual direction

Keep PocketPet's pastel bedroom, pixel pup, and glossy illustrated UI. Orange means hunger, pink happiness, purple energy, and mint cleanliness. Use readable action names and keep controls away from the pet.

Use the generated status frames and separate fills. Keep the room visible without putting important controls directly on busy background details.

The care screen uses a 720-by-1280 portrait reference. Status bars form two rows at the top. Eight action buttons form two rows of four at the bottom, with matching glossy pill shapes, distinct colors, and action-specific pups.

Feed is orange, Love pink, Play green, Study blue, Sleep purple, Wash turquoise, Drink yellow, and Scene coral. A cream speech bubble appears above the main pet during an action. Props stay near the pet and disappear at idle.
