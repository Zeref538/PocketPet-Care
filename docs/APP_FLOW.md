# Play flow

Open the care scene. The pup idles in its bedroom. Choose a care action to play its animation and sound and show its speech bubble. The supplied script returns to idle after three seconds. Another press restarts that timer.

Scene toggles between bedroom and garden, preserves current bar values, and briefly shows scenery speech. It does not load another Unity scene. Study, sleep, and feed props disappear when the pet returns to idle; wash and drink props are part of their animation frames.

Status feedback is session-only. Exiting Play mode resets the scene. No save or sickness screen is promised.
